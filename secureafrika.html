The Model

Two disciplines, one system

SecureAfrika does not deliver aid. It transfers a method — agronomic and managerial — designed to keep running after the founding team steps back.

01 — The Land

Syntropic Agroforestry

Degraded or underused land is replanted in dense, multi-layered systems that mimic natural forest succession — trees, shrubs, and crops grown together so the land regenerates its own fertility instead of depleting it. The result is food, timber, and income from the same acre, indefinitely.

02 — The Institution

American Business Leadership

Every planted site is paired with a management discipline drawn from Western enterprise practice — governance, planning, execution — so the project outlives its founders and can be handed to local operators as a running concern, not a dependency.

The Path

How the first year is funded

Four stages, in sequence. Each stage is designed to fund and de-risk the one after it.

01

Founding Network

A direct crowdfunding push to the founders' own professional and personal networks — covers incorporation costs, the initial site visit to the DRC, and this website.

Target: seed capital for setup + first site visit
02

Grant Applications

Restoration- and agriculture-specific grant bodies — programs aligned with land restoration and smallholder agriculture across Africa.

Target: land restoration & agriculture-focused grant funding
03

Fiscal Sponsorship

Partnership with an established restoration NGO provides credibility, matched funding, and a legal path to accept larger gifts before SecureAfrika's own 501(c)(3) is finalized.

Target: matched funding + institutional credibility
04

Charter

Delaware incorporation and 501(c)(3) status are completed, and a dedicated nonprofit bank account opens — the point at which SecureAfrika can operate, and fundraise, on its own standing.

Milestone: full legal independence

Substantive Chief of Staff to British Columbia Premier David Eby, appointed in November 2024 after the provincial election, on medical leave since January 2026 following a cancer diagnosis disclosed by the Office of the Premier ([Government of British Columbia](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026PREM0002-000029)). His leave was extended in July 2026, at which point Andrew Mercier was appointed to fill the role and acting chief of staff Cheryl Oates concluded her term appointment ([Government of British Columbia](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026PREM0048-000851)). Previously chief of staff to B.C. finance minister Carole James and principal secretary to Alberta premier Rachel Notley.

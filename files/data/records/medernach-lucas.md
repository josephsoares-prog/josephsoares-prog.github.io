Lucas Medernach returned to the Parti Québécois as director of communications on March 23, 2026, six months before the Quebec general election ([Parti Québécois](https://pq.org/nouvelles/lucas-medernach-revient-au-parti-quebecois-a-titre-de-directeur-des-communications/)). He had spent the previous four years as director of communications in the office of the Bloc Québécois leader in the House of Commons, and most recently as director of communications and marketing at the Fédération des chambres de commerce du Québec. He began at the PQ in 2016 and held the same communications-director post there in 2020-21. DG Génifère Legrand announced the hire.

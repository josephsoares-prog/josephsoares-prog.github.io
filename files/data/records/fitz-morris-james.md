James Fitz-Morris is listed as Director of Communications in the Office of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Global Affairs Canada, in the Government of Canada's official GEDS directory as of August 28, 2026 ([GEDS](https://geds-sage.gc.ca/en/GEDS?pgid=015&dn=Q049RklUWk1PUlJJU1wyQyBKQU1FUyxPVT1NSU5BLU1JTkEsT1U9R0FDLUFNQyxPPUdDLEM9Q0E%3D)). He took over as communications director to then-Public Services and Procurement Minister Anita Anand in June 2020 ([The Hill Times](https://www.hilltimes.com/story/2020/06/03/fitz-morris-takes-over-as-communications-director-to-pspc-minister-anand/280249/)), and before entering ministerial staff he covered federal politics from CBC's Parliament Hill bureau from 2006 until February 2016 ([CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/author/james-fitz-morris-1.1870797)).

Katy Merrifield served as Director of Communications to Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre in the Office of the Leader of the Opposition. Her resignation was reported on April 8, 2026 by The Canadian Press and carried by CTV News, The Globe and Mail and Village Media outlets ([CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/politics/article/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/); [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/canada/article-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/)).

Her exit was the first of several senior departures from the OLO across 2026 that the Files tracks separately — chief of staff Ian Todd's retirement and the arrival of Steve Outhouse and Aaron Wudrick in the top two chairs.

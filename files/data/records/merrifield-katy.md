Katy Merrifield served as Director of Communications to Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre in the Office of the Leader of the Opposition. Her resignation was reported on April 8, 2026 by The Canadian Press and carried by CTV News, The Globe and Mail, CP24 and Village Media outlets ([CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/politics/article/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/); [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/canada/article-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/); [CP24](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/04/08/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/)).

She announced the departure in an email to the Conservative caucus, naming Micah Green as the incoming communications director and Sam Lilly as the incoming media relations director, and stating that both promotions were her own suggestion - an internal succession she designed on the way out.

Her exit was the first of several senior departures from the OLO across 2026 that the Files tracks separately - chief of staff Ian Todd's retirement and the arrival of Steve Outhouse and Aaron Wudrick in the top two chairs.

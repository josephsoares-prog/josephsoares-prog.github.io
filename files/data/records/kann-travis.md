Travis Kann was appointed Chief of Staff to Ontario Premier Doug Ford, effective March 6, 2026, succeeding Patrick Sackville, who had held the role for eight years and stayed on through the transition ([QP Briefing](https://www.qpbriefing.com/news/doug-ford-names-travis-kann-new-chief-of-staff-as-sackville-leaves); [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/politics/article-doug-ford-chief-of-staff-canada-us-relations/)). Kann previously served as principal secretary and head of Ontario-U.S. engagement in the Premier's Office, having joined government through the Ministry of Health in 2020. In July 2026, his office eliminated 10 positions and initiated a hiring freeze, projected to save Ontario taxpayers roughly $1.07 million annually ([CP24](https://www.cp24.com/politics/queens-park/2026/07/16/ontario-premiers-office-slashes-headcount-initiates-hiring-freeze-to-save-money/)).

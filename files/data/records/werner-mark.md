Managed Peter Milobar's campaigns from 2016 until stepping back in April 2026 amid an Elections BC investigation into a 2024 website and mail-out targeting then-leader John Rustad and MLA Teresa Wat — Elections BC fined BC United $4,500 and reporting states its evidence named Werner as the campaign manager who organized the materials ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/milobar-campaign-manager-shake-up-elections-b-c-investigation-9.7160655); [Castanet](https://www.castanetkamloops.net/news/Kamloops/608518/Kamloops-MLA-Milobar-says-campaign-manager-s-exit-is-coincidence-amid-dirty-trick-controversy)). Milobar called the departure's timing "a little suspect" but attributed it to Werner's family business. Succeeded by Jeff Conaster.

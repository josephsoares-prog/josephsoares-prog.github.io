Louise Soucy was named executive director of the Parti conservateur du Québec in January 2023, replacing Raffael Cavaliere ([Le Devoir](https://www.ledevoir.com/politique/quebec/778364/une-ex-candidate-pequiste-nommee-directrice-du-parti-conservateur-du-quebec)). She had been campaign director for PCQ candidate Frédéric Poulin in Côte-du-Sud in the 2022 general election, and had herself run as a Parti Québécois candidate in Montmagny-L'Islet in 2003. A career manager in the food and health sectors around Montmagny, her hiring was presented by the party as a step toward professionalising the organisation after its disappointing October 2022 result ([Le Devoir](https://www.ledevoir.com/politique/quebec/790261/politique-quebecoise-divorce-consacre-entre-le-pcq-et-son-ancien-directeur)). No source dated 2026 confirming she still holds the post was found as of this record's verification date.

Micah Green became Director of Communications to Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre in April 2026, promoted from within the Office of the Leader of the Opposition on the resignation of Katy Merrifield ([CTV News / The Canadian Press](https://www.ctvnews.ca/politics/article/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/); [CP24](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/04/08/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/)).

The move was announced in Merrifield's own departure email to the Conservative caucus, in which she said she had recommended both her successor and the simultaneous promotion of Sam Lilly to media relations director - an internal handover rather than an external hire, at a point in 2026 when the leader's office was replacing its senior ranks.

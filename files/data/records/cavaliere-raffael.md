Raffael Cavaliere was directeur général of the Parti conservateur du Québec through the party's 2021-22 growth period and held an operational role in its 2022 general election campaign ([Le Devoir](https://www.ledevoir.com/politique/quebec/790261/politique-quebecoise-divorce-consacre-entre-le-pcq-et-son-ancien-directeur)). A computer programmer and musician from the greater Montreal area, he joined Éric Duhaime's party in reaction to pandemic health measures in schools and co-founded the Parents Québec collective in 2021. He was replaced as DG by Louise Soucy in January 2023 and initially stayed on as vice-president for the Montérégie and Estrie regions ([Le Devoir](https://www.ledevoir.com/politique/quebec/778364/une-ex-candidate-pequiste-nommee-directrice-du-parti-conservateur-du-quebec)), before leaving the organisation in March 2023 amid tensions settled by a confidential agreement.

Patrick Sackville served as Chief of Staff to Ontario Premier Doug Ford from December 2022 until March 2026, a tenure Global News described as "more than three years at the top," spanning the fallout from the Greenbelt scandal and an early provincial election ([Global News, Mar 2, 2026](https://globalnews.ca/news/11713439/doug-ford-travis-kann-new-chief-staff/)).

On March 2, 2026, the Premier's Office confirmed that principal secretary Travis Kann would take over as chief of staff. Sackville remained in post until the end of that week and then departed government ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11713439/doug-ford-travis-kann-new-chief-staff/); [QP Briefing](https://www.qpbriefing.com/news/doug-ford-names-travis-kann-new-chief-of-staff-as-sackville-leaves)).

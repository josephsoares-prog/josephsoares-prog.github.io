Former Harper communications director and three-time Ford campaign manager. Publicly critical of the 2025 federal campaign run by Jenni Byrne; in 2026 backed Caroline Elliott in the BC Conservative leadership contest alongside Nick Kouvalis — a race won by Kerry-Lynne Findlay, with Elliott second (~49%) — drawing a public jab from Poilievre at the Calgary Stampede ([National Observer, Jul 9, 2026](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/07/09/news/bc-conservative-candidate-tells-poilievre-unite-not-divide-after-stampede-jab); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11874270/new-leader-bc-conservative-party/)). Fired back publicly on the "Curse of Politics" podcast, calling for a "wellness check" on Poilievre — cited by Global News (Jul 14, 2026) amid reporting of Conservative fundraising worries and internal pressure on the leader ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11965637/conservatives-fear-fundraising-slump/)).

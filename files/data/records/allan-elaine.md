Elaine Allan, a Vancouver nonprofit executive and the Conservative Party's 2025 federal candidate for Vancouver Centre, became executive director of the Conservative Party of British Columbia after Angelo Isidorou's July 30, 2026 resignation ([Black Press Media](https://grandforksgazette.ca/2026/09/04/how-the-b-c-conservatives-went-from-rising-force-to-party-in-turmoil/)). Her tenure was brief: the Western Standard reported August 29, 2026 that she had been relieved of the position after a dispute over a data download from party systems — which she said was meant to stop Conservative data from disappearing — with she and a staffer placed on precautionary suspension pending a committee review ([Western Standard](https://www.westernstandard.news/bc/breaking-findlays-executive-director-relieved-after-mailbox-standoff/76478)).

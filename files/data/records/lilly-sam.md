Sam Lilly became Director of Media Relations in the Office of the Leader of the Opposition in April 2026, promoted from within alongside Micah Green when communications director Katy Merrifield resigned ([CTV News / The Canadian Press](https://www.ctvnews.ca/politics/article/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/); [CP24](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/04/08/pierre-poilievres-communications-director-katy-merrifield-resigns/)).

Merrifield told the Conservative caucus that both promotions were her own suggestion, making the pair an internal continuation of the communications shop rather than a change of direction imposed from outside it.

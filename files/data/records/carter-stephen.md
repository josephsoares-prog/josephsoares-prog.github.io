Veteran Alberta political strategist and president of his own firm, Decide Campaigns — architect of Naheed Nenshi's 2010 Calgary mayoral win, an adviser to former Alberta premier Alison Redford, National Director of Campaign Strategy at Hill+Knowlton Strategies (2012-2015), and a leader of Jyoti Gondek's Calgary mayoral campaign — hired by the Manitoba Progressive Conservatives to run their campaign for the next provincial election ([Global News/CP](https://globalnews.ca/news/11564451/stephen-carter-manitoba-election-campaign/); [Decide Campaigns](https://www.decidecampaigns.com/team)).

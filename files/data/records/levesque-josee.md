Josée Lévesque is campaign director for the Quebec Liberal Party under leader Charles Milliard, presented alongside chief of staff Marie-Pier Richard when Milliard was introduced to party members in Trois-Rivières on February 15, 2026 ([Le Soleil / Le Nouvelliste](https://www.lesoleil.com/actualites/2026/02/15/le-plq-accueille-avec-engouement-son-nouveau-chef-charles-milliard-HGNO3LW2G5HLREPKIGX24ZZNDM/)). A longtime PLQ activist, she held the same role for Philippe Couillard in the 2014 election that brought him to power and in the 2018 election he lost to François Legault.

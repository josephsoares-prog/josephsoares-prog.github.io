Fred DeLorey ran the Conservative Party's 2021 national campaign and previously held political-operations roles for Doug Ford and Stephen Harper; he is now chair and chief strategy officer of NorthStar Public Affairs, working as a consultant-lobbyist for corporate clients ([NorthStar, official bio](https://northstarpa.ca/person/fred)). In July 2026 he re-entered the public conversation on Conservative unity, calling intraparty infighting "clearly a problem" amid the dispute between Pierre Poilievre and BC Conservative-aligned strategists Kory Teneycke and Caroline Elliott ([National Post via Yahoo News Canada, Jul 9, 2026](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/former-b-c-conservative-leadership-175418654.html)). By September 2026 his warnings had sharpened: "The leader's not leading. We're all over the place. Our message isn't consistent, there's infighting, there's all these different factions" ([The Canadian Press via Energeticcity.ca, Sep 9, 2026](https://energeticcity.ca/2026/09/09/as-the-trade-war-dominates-canadian-politics-conservatives-fight-among-themselves/)).

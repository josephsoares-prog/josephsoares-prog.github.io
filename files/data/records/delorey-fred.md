Fred DeLorey ran the Conservative Party's 2021 national campaign and previously held political-operations roles for Doug Ford and Stephen Harper; he is now chair and chief strategy officer of NorthStar Public Affairs, working as a consultant-lobbyist for corporate clients ([NorthStar, official bio](https://northstarpa.ca/person/fred)). In July 2026 he re-entered the public conversation on Conservative unity, calling intraparty infighting "clearly a problem" amid the dispute between Pierre Poilievre and BC Conservative-aligned strategists Kory Teneycke and Caroline Elliott ([National Post via Yahoo News Canada, Jul 9, 2026](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/former-b-c-conservative-leadership-175418654.html)).

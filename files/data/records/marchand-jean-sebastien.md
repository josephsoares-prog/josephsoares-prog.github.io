Jean-Sébastien Marchand was the Parti Québécois's directeur général until August 2023 — the youngest in the party's history, per the party — and managed its 2022 general election campaign before being named conseiller spécial with strategic, political and diplomatic responsibilities in preparation for the 2026 election ([Parti Québécois](https://pq.org/nouvelles/deux-nouvelles-nominations-dans-les-hautes-spheres-du-parti-quebecois/)). He was succeeded as DG by Génifère Legrand. No public source confirming his role after the August 2023 announcement was found as of this record's verification date.

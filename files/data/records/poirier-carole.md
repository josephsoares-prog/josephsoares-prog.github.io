Carole Poirier returned to the Parti Québécois in November 2025 as the party's head of candidate recruitment for the 2026 election campaign, announced jointly by DG Génifère Legrand and leader Paul St-Pierre Plamondon ([Parti Québécois](https://pq.org/nouvelles/le-parti-quebecois-annonce-le-retour-de-carole-poirier/)). She was chief of staff to Louise Harel from 1997 to 2006, MNA for Hochelaga-Maisonneuve from 2008 to 2018, a former directrice générale of the PQ, and until recently chief of staff to Terrebonne mayor Mathieu Traversy.

Daniel Minden was named deputy chief of staff to Foreign Affairs Minister Anita Anand in August 2026 ([The Hill Times](https://www.hilltimes.com/2026/08/27/deputy-chief-of-staff-added-to-foreign-minister-anands-team/515945/)), and is listed in the Office of the Minister of Foreign Affairs in the Government of Canada's official GEDS directory as of August 28, 2026 ([GEDS](https://geds-sage.gc.ca/en/GEDS/?pgid=014&dn=T1U9TUlOQS1NSU5BLE9VPUdBQy1BTUMsTz1HQyxDPUNB)). He previously served as Press Secretary and Senior Communications Advisor in the Office of the Minister of National Defence under then-Defence Minister Anand ([National Defence](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/news/2023/06/defence-minister-anita-anand-announces-revamped-indo-pacific-military-mission-and-strengthens-canadas-defence-relationships-in-the-region.html)).

Longtime Conservative operative who worked as a Senior Ontario Desk Officer at CPC headquarters, an Issues Manager in Stephen Harper's PMO, and Principal Secretary and Director of Outreach to Erin O'Toole as Leader of the Opposition (2020-2022) before serving as Deputy National Campaign Manager for the CPC's 2021 federal campaign; now a Senior Vice President at Vantage (McMillan Vantage) in Ottawa ([Vantage Canada, official bio](https://vantagecanada.com/team); [Canada Newswire, Oct 17, 2024](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/seasoned-conservative-strategist-laura-kurkimaki-joins-mcmillan-vantage-s-ottawa-team-823294729.html)). Quoted in a September 2026 Canadian Press wire story on Conservative Party internal divisions, alongside Fred DeLorey, Cole Hogan and Ginny Roth ([The Canadian Press via Energeticcity.ca, Sep 9, 2026](https://energeticcity.ca/2026/09/09/as-the-trade-war-dominates-canadian-politics-conservatives-fight-among-themselves/)).

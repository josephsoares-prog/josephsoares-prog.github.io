Génifère Legrand has been directrice générale of the Parti Québécois since August 7, 2023, when the party announced her arrival and moved outgoing DG Jean-Sébastien Marchand to a special-adviser role ([Parti Québécois](https://pq.org/nouvelles/deux-nouvelles-nominations-dans-les-hautes-spheres-du-parti-quebecois/)). She came from C2 International and C2 Montréal, where she was executive vice-president and chief creative officer. She remains the party's DG through the 2026 election cycle, appearing in that capacity in the party's November 2025 announcement of Carole Poirier's return ([PQ](https://pq.org/nouvelles/le-parti-quebecois-annonce-le-retour-de-carole-poirier/)) and in the March 2026 announcement of Lucas Medernach as director of communications ([PQ](https://pq.org/nouvelles/lucas-medernach-revient-au-parti-quebecois-a-titre-de-directeur-des-communications/)).

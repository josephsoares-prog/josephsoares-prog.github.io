Isabelle Sabourin is the Quebec Liberal Party's candidate in Papineau for the 2026 Quebec general election, nominated on June 22, 2026 in Ripon alongside QLP leader Charles Milliard ([Parti liberal du Quebec](https://plq.org/en/isabelle-sabourin-the-quebec-liberal-partys-official-candidate-in-papineau/)) and confirmed on the regional candidate list for the riding ([TVA Gatineau](https://tvagatineau.ca/elections-provinciales-vos-candidats/)). The campaign has been underway since Premier Christine Frechette dissolved the National Assembly on August 27, 2026, with the vote set for October 5 ([CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/montreal/quebec-election-campaign-launch-9.7321417)). The PLQ release records that since 2021 she served as Director of Strategic and Regional Issues in the office of Canada's Minister of Transport and Government Leader in the House of Commons, and that she previously served as a policy advisor to the National Assembly of Quebec and as a territorial director for the City of Gatineau — a career that crosses all three orders of government within the Outaouais.

Isabelle Sabourin was officially nominated as the Quebec Liberal Party's candidate in Papineau on June 22, 2026, in Ripon, alongside QLP leader Charles Milliard ([Parti liberal du Quebec](https://plq.org/en/isabelle-sabourin-the-quebec-liberal-partys-official-candidate-in-papineau/)). The same release records that since 2021 she has served as Director of Strategic and Regional Issues in the office of Canada's Minister of Transport and Government Leader in the House of Commons, and that she previously served as a policy advisor to the National Assembly of Quebec and as a territorial director for the City of Gatineau — a career that crosses all three orders of government within the Outaouais.

Acting Chief of Staff to British Columbia Premier David Eby from January 2026, appointed for the duration of substantive chief of staff Jim Rutkowski's medical leave ([Government of British Columbia](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026PREM0002-000029)). Her term appointment concluded in July 2026 when Rutkowski's leave was extended and Andrew Mercier was appointed to fill the role ([Government of British Columbia](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026PREM0048-000851)). Previously chief communications adviser to Alberta premier Rachel Notley and provincial campaign director in the 2024 Saskatchewan provincial election; teaches political communications at McGill University's Max Bell School of Public Policy. On September 16, 2026, Premier Eby named her campaign manager for the B.C. NDP's next provincial election, saying the party needs to be "election ready" amid snap-election speculation ([The Canadian Press via CHEK News](https://cheknews.ca/eby-says-ndp-needs-to-be-election-ready-in-b-c-but-offers-no-date-1348214/)).

Acting Chief of Staff to British Columbia Premier David Eby from January 2026, appointed for the duration of substantive chief of staff Jim Rutkowski's medical leave ([Government of British Columbia](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026PREM0002-000029)). Her term appointment concluded in July 2026 when Rutkowski's leave was extended and Andrew Mercier was appointed to fill the role ([Government of British Columbia](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026PREM0048-000851)). Previously chief communications adviser to Alberta premier Rachel Notley and provincial campaign director in the 2024 Saskatchewan provincial election; teaches political communications at McGill University's Max Bell School of Public Policy.

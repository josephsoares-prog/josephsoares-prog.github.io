Campaign manager and executive director credited with much of the BC Conservative Party's resurgence from marginal polling to Official Opposition status, stepping down July 30, 2026 on what he and new leader Kerry-Lynne Findlay both described as amicable terms ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/angelo-isidorou-bc-conservatives-steps-down-9.7291070)). His departure opened a wider executive shakeup that continued into August 2026 with the replacement of party president Aisha Estey and vice-president Sharon White ([The Canadian Press/Energeticcity.ca](https://energeticcity.ca/2026/08/20/aisha-estey-out-as-b-c-conservative-president-as-shakeup-continues/)).

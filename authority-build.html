How the work is done.

Every piece is written by people, from your own words. We interview you in writing or by voice note, draft, and send it to you for approval. Nothing is published under your name that you have not read.

The setup comes first because most senior people have no platform of their own: a LinkedIn profile, perhaps an old bio on a former employer’s site. We audit what exists, settle how you should be described in one sentence, and build a website you own.

After that the work is steady rather than loud: a regular article, a LinkedIn presence that sounds like you, and a newsletter to the people who should hear from you. In the Full tier we also keep the website current, produce video, and position you for media and speaking.

We work in English first. Our team is multilingual, so where your audience needs it we publish equivalent content in French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, Russian and Ukrainian.

The book is a separate project, priced on its own, and is not part of either tier.

Who it is for

Senior people at a turning point: a board search, life after public office, a book, an advisory practice being launched. People whose record is stronger than their footprint.

Who it is not for

Anyone who wants volume, viral reach or a new persona. We make the real record visible. We do not invent one.

Why U.S. Energy Independence Is a 9/11 Legacy

September 11, 2026 · Dispatch · By Joseph Soares

Twenty-five years after the September 11 attacks, the durable legacy is not the wars and not the airports. It is a routine administrative power to cut an institution out of the dollar system — created in 2001, given a permanent office in 2004 — and an energy position that inverted over the same quarter-century.

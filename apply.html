The 30-Day SPARK Sprint

Apply for the next cohort

Six places per cohort. Applications are read in the order they arrive.

I read every application myself. If you are a fit you will get an acceptance and a payment link within three business days. If you are not, you will get a reply that says so and why — I would rather tell you in writing than take your money and find out in week two.

There is no call in this process. That is intentional. What you write below tells me more than a conversation would, and it leaves you a record of your own thinking before the Sprint begins.

Before you start
This takes about fifteen minutes.

It is meant to. The three long questions below are the application — the rest is administration. A careless answer to any of them is a decline, so give yourself the time.

Your application is confidential and is never used as content, in any form.

Application received.

I read them in order and reply within three business days, either way. If I need anything clarified I will ask by email.

In the meantime: if the decision is urgent and you would rather not wait for a cohort, the Pressure Read turns it around in five business days.

What happens after you submit

Three business days to a decision, either way. An acceptance comes with a payment link and the cohort date. A decline comes with the specific reason and what I would suggest instead.

Not ready for a cohort?

The Pressure Read takes one decision and returns it in writing in five business days. Or take the assessment first — eleven minutes, free.

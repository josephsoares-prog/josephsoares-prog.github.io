What you get
Three things you can act on.

1See what they see

What a board chair, a journalist and a prospective client each find on the first page of results for your name, in English first and in any other language your audience reads. Screenshots included. What is missing, stale or wrong.

Who is reading it

Most of the senior people we work with are better than their search results. Their best work happened in rooms the public never saw, and they have no time, and no appetite, to become content producers.

How it works
Three steps.
Step 1

Apply for your Authority Read

Apply below. I choose the people I take each month and reply in writing within five business days. If I choose you, a short written questionnaire opens. It takes about thirty minutes. No CV? That’s fine: many of the people we work with have never needed one. We do a short call instead and put it together with you.

Step 2

We read your record and your search results

We check your answers against what the search shows today, in English first and in any other language that matters to you.

Step 3

The Read arrives, in writing and on video

Within five business days: the written document, plus a video briefing of eight minutes or less that walks you through it. Reread it, rewatch it, and share both with the people who advise you.

Taking the Read commits you to nothing else. Nothing you send is used as content, now or later. If something is unclear, we ask in writing.

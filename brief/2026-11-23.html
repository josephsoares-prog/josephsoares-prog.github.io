GAZA CEASEFIRE

Israel Ends Its Rare Central Gaza Ground Operation as Families Return to Nuseirat

Al-Monitor (Reuters, AP) · al-monitor.com ↗

The Israeli military declared its five-day ground push into the Nuseirat area of central Gaza complete and began withdrawing tanks, letting displaced families return, after the incursion was launched over a ceasefire violation and briefly became the largest breach of the truce signed over a year ago. The fact that Israel could launch and then unwind a tank operation inside the supposed ceasefire zone without the deal collapsing outright tells decision-makers the truce's real floor is lower than its text suggests — treat any further ‘rare’ incursion as the new baseline risk, not an aberration.

GEOPOLITICS

Trump-Xi-Putin 'Peace Declaration' Leaves Out Ukraine and Iran, Leaving Both Wars Exactly Where They Were

Axios · axios.com ↗

The first-ever Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral in Shenzhen produced a broad declaration on nuclear risk reduction and energy supply but, by design, said nothing about ending the wars in Ukraine or with Iran — the two conflicts investors and allies most need resolved. Four days on, that omission is proving to be the real signal: the summit bought a diplomatic photo op without committing any of the three powers to the harder trade-offs still blocking both the Ukraine peace framework and the Hormuz standoff, so neither should be priced as closer to resolution than before the summit.

VENEZUELA

US and Venezuela Sign Deal to Rebuild Military Ties as Hegseth Visits Caracas's Interim Leaders

Al Jazeera · aljazeera.com ↗

Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth signed an agreement to re-establish US-Venezuela military cooperation during a visit to Caracas's interim authorities, the latest and most concrete step in formalising a post-Maduro security relationship weeks after Washington's operation to remove him. For firms weighing re-entry into Venezuelan energy and logistics, a signed military pact is a stronger signal of durable US commitment to the interim government than rhetoric alone — but the absence of a named elected successor still keeps political risk elevated.

UKRAINE

US Floats Ukraine Ceding Donbas in Exchange for a Path to NATO Membership, Zelenskyy Says

NBC News · nbcnews.com ↗

Volodymyr Zelenskyy said US negotiators have floated a framework in which Ukraine would cede the remainder of the Donbas in return for a credible path to NATO membership, a trade that would hand Russia the territorial win it has sought since 2014 while giving Kyiv the security guarantee it has sought even longer. Any version of this that advances will reprice Eastern European defence spending, sanctions-relief timelines and Russian sovereign-risk exposure simultaneously, so treat the specific sequencing — territory first or membership first — as the variable that actually matters, not the headline trade itself.

UKRAINE

European Allies Push Back on US-Russia Peace Plan, Demand a Seat Before Any Deal Is Final

The Star (AFP, Reuters) · thestar.com.my ↗

European governments are pushing back on a US-drafted peace framework they say leans toward Moscow's preferred terms, insisting any final settlement cannot be struck over Kyiv's and Europe's heads given European forces and financing would underwrite whatever security guarantee Ukraine gets. For NATO members and defence contractors, the practical read is that Washington's timeline for closing a deal is now contingent on a second, separate negotiation with European capitals — add that layer of friction to any planning horizon built on a quick signature.

TECHNOLOGY & AI

US Closes China's Cloud-Rental Loophole for Nvidia's Top AI Chips

South China Morning Post · scmp.com ↗

The US Commerce Department tightened export-control rules to stop Chinese firms from accessing Nvidia's most advanced AI chips by renting cloud capacity through overseas data centres in third countries, closing a workaround that had let restricted compute keep flowing despite direct export bans. For any multinational running AI workloads through regional cloud providers, the compliance perimeter just expanded from 'where the chip is' to 'where the renting entity is controlled from' — audit third-country cloud contracts accordingly before year-end.

Wall Street's AI-Stock Volatility Drags On as Tech Shares Surrender Early Gains

Anadolu Agency · aa.com.tr ↗

US equities closed mixed again as technology shares gave up early-session gains, extending a stretch of whiplash trading in AI-exposed names that has run alongside Nvidia's leadership vacuum and renewed doubts about AI-infrastructure spending discipline. With no permanent Nvidia CEO yet named and chip-export rules tightening in parallel, portfolio managers should expect the sector's volatility to stay elevated into December rather than settle, since both of its near-term catalysts remain unresolved.

ENERGY & OIL

OPEC+ Holds December Output Steady, Punting the Harder Call to 2027

OPEC · opec.org ↗

The OPEC+ subgroup of Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman kept December production quotas unchanged, reaffirming a commitment to market stability rather than testing the group's cohesion with another increase heading into a winter demand season already clouded by surplus forecasts. The decision buys the group time but does not resolve the underlying tension between members wanting higher output now and a market that keeps pricing in oversupply — expect that fight to resurface at the first 2027 meeting.

ENERGY & OIL

Oil Markets Keep Discounting Middle East Shipping Risk Even as Attacks Continue

Khaleej Times · khaleejtimes.com ↗

Crude prices have stayed rangebound as forecasts of a widening global supply surplus keep outweighing the physical risk from ongoing Middle East shipping attacks, including the unresolved Hormuz standoff with Iran — a disconnect between the headline barrel price and the risk actually on the water that has persisted for weeks. Hedging desks and shippers should treat that gap, not the price level itself, as the thing to watch: a single confirmed disruption to a major export corridor could reprice crude faster than the surplus narrative currently allows for.

Prologis (investor relations) · ir.prologis.com ↗

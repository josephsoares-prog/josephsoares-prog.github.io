Commentary Hooks

Plan for the closed strait, not the reopening

Most 2027 plans still assume Hormuz reopens on a diplomatic timetable. Tehran has now priced reopening in regional-war terms, so the honest planning case is a strait that stays shut and a freight market that has already repriced.

When your ally becomes your auditor

The Pentagon is scoring allied contribution before deciding where troops leave. Any partnership that turns conditional works the same way: contribution has to be documented in the partner s own terms, not asserted in yours.

The exemption list is the real trade policy

Ottawa removed seafood from its counter-tariff list because an industry brought numbers. The headline rate makes news, but the line-item list decides who pays, and it is written by whoever shows up with evidence before the deadline.

AI & POLITICS

“What We Just Witnessed Was an AI Earthquake”: Voters Revolt Against Data-Center Politics at the Polls

Axios · axios.com ↗

Axios reports that anger over AI data-center buildouts — rising electricity bills, water use and land fights — became a live issue in Tuesday’s midterms, with candidates who ran against the buildout outperforming in several contested races. This is the first US election in which the AI infrastructure boom itself, not just AI jobs or safety, was clearly on the ballot. For any company breaking ground on a data center, local political risk just became as material as power availability or chip supply.

ELON MUSK

Elon Musk Suffers a Political Setback in the 2026 Midterms as AI Data-Center Stocks Wobble

CNBC · cnbc.com ↗

CNBC reports that Tuesday’s results dealt Elon Musk a personal political setback, compounding a session in which AI data-center-linked stocks declined alongside renewed scrutiny of the sector’s political standing. The read-through is that Musk’s political capital — which he has used to back candidates and policy fights tied to AI and energy — is no longer a one-way bet. For investors tracking Musk-linked equities, his political exposure is now a variable analysts have to model, not a constant.

AI INFRASTRUCTURE

OpenAI Pledges to Pay for Its Own Data-Center Power After the Election Exposes Public Anxiety About AI

CNBC · cnbc.com ↗

CNBC reports that OpenAI has pledged to cover the power costs its data centers impose, a direct response to the political backlash crystallized in Tuesday’s vote over who ultimately pays for the AI buildout’s electricity demand. The pledge concedes the core complaint — that ordinary ratepayers are subsidizing AI infrastructure — without yet detailing enforcement or cost. For utilities and regulators negotiating data-center interconnection deals, OpenAI has just reset the opening ask in every future negotiation.

MARKETS

Apple and Other Big Tech Stocks Surge After the 2026 Midterm Elections

CNBC · cnbc.com ↗

CNBC reports that Apple and a cluster of large technology names rallied after Tuesday’s results, as investors judged the outcome to lower certain Washington policy risks for consumer-facing tech even as AI-infrastructure names traded more cautiously. The divergence from Musk-linked and data-center stocks the same day shows the market treating “big tech” as two separate trades, not one. For portfolio managers, index-level tech exposure now masks a meaningful split that calls for stock-level, not sector-level, positioning.

AI TRADE

The Post-Election Rally Masks a Sharp Split in the AI Trade — Who Stands to Win?

CNBC · cnbc.com ↗

CNBC’s Investing Club notes that the broad post-election relief rally obscures a sharpening divide within AI-exposed equities, between companies seen as insulated from the data-center backlash and those directly exposed to it. The piece frames this as the market beginning to price political risk into individual AI names rather than the theme as a whole. For allocators who have been running the “AI trade” as a single basket, that basket just became considerably harder to hold as one position.

OpenAI Targets Microsoft’s GitHub, in Talks to Buy a Code-Hosting Startup

Axios · axios.com ↗

For enterprise software buyers, the Microsoft-OpenAI relationship just became a rivalry investors need to track line by line, not a settled alliance.

CANADA-US

Doug Ford Calls the Democratic Sweep a ‘Victory for Canada’ as the Trade War With Washington Persists

The Globe and Mail · theglobeandmail.com ↗

The Globe and Mail reports that Ontario Premier Doug Ford called Tuesday’s Democratic sweep of Congress a “victory for Canada,” part of live coverage tracking how the midterm results are being read in Ottawa and the provinces as the Canada-US trade war continues. A more adversarial Congress doesn’t itself remove Canadian tariffs, but it changes the domestic political backdrop against which Carney’s negotiators and provincial premiers are operating. For Canadian exporters, the terrain just shifted — the outcome is encouraging, but it is not a settlement.

MARKETS

European Stocks Hit a Fresh Record as the AI Unwind Boosts the Region

Bloomberg · bloomberg.com ↗

Bloomberg reports that European equities touched a fresh record Thursday as capital rotated out of AI-exposed names and into the region’s value and cyclical stocks — the same anxiety showing up in US data-center politics is now a live capital-allocation trade in global markets. The Bank of England Is Poised for Its First Rate Hike in Three Years

Bloomberg · bloomberg.com ↗

Bloomberg’s decision guide frames the Bank of England as poised to raise its policy rate for the first time in three years, which would make it an outlier among G7 central banks still holding or cutting. For anyone running cross-currency carry trades or UK rate-sensitive exposure, today’s decision is the one to watch, not a formality.

Bloomberg · bloomberg.com ↗

Bloomberg reports that the International Finance Corporation’s $22 billion push to deploy capital across African markets is being constrained by shallow local capital markets that limit how quickly the money can actually be put to work.

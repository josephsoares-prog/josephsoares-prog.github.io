Commentary Hooks

The Pause Trap: why a ceasefire you can cancel is worse than no ceasefire at all

Markets price a pause as peace, but a truce that one side denies and the other can end at will is a leverage instrument, not a settlement. The discipline is to keep hedges on through the calm — because the calm is the trade, and the trade can be revoked.

Capex as Confession: how the AI trade flipped from spend-to-win to protect-the-cash

In one quarter, the market stopped rewarding AI buildout and started rewarding restraint — Apple over Nvidia, Alphabet down 7% on capex. The leadership question is no longer who spends most on AI, but who can show a return before free cash flow turns negative.

One Theatre, Two Wars: reading Ukraine and Iran as a single connected system

Drone swarms over Moscow, a Russia-Iran logistics corridor and a NATO border closing airspace are not separate stories — they share supply chains, tactics and choke points. Leaders who still run these as two risk registers are budgeting reaction time they no longer have.

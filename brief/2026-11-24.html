TECHNOLOGY & AI

Nvidia's Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, Citing Government Input on National Security

NVIDIA (SEC filing) · sec.gov ↗

NVIDIA's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director effective immediately, eleven days after ousting him amid a foreign-intelligence-targeting inquiry, saying it had received input from senior U.S. officials and was satisfied his return “poses no national security risk”; interim CEO Colette Kress reverts to CFO, and a new National Security and Export Control Committee and Chief Compliance Officer post have been created. A boardroom reversal this fast, with the White House's fingerprints on the national-security sign-off, tells decision-makers that Nvidia's governance is now effectively co-managed with Washington — price that into any assumption about the company's independence on export-control decisions going forward.

MARKETS & MACRO

Wall Street Stages Its Sharpest Rally in Months as Huang's Return Ignites a Chip-Stock Rebound

Fox Business (Reuters) · foxbusiness.com ↗

The Dow jumped 1,447.98 points (3.01%) to 49,595.31, the S&P 500 rose 4.73% and the Nasdaq 6.51% on Monday as Nvidia surged more than 31% on Huang's reinstatement, with Micron up 19%, AMD up over 6% and data-centre names CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN all up 20% or more, reversing a correction that had erased the Nasdaq's 2026 gain. For portfolio managers, a single leadership announcement driving Wall Street's best session in roughly seven months confirms just how concentrated and sentiment-driven the AI trade has become — treat the rebound as a reprieve from, not a reversal of, that fragility.

BUSINESS & M&A

CrowdStrike Agrees to Acquire Zscaler for $43.9 Billion in the Largest Cybersecurity Deal on Record

CrowdStrike (SEC filing) · sec.gov ↗

CrowdStrike will acquire Zscaler in an all-stock deal valuing it at roughly $43.9 billion, with each Zscaler share converting into 1.135 CrowdStrike shares, merging endpoint and identity security with Zero Trust network defences and targeting $1 billion in annualized revenue synergies within 30 months. For Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet and every other Zero Trust vendor, the largest cybersecurity merger on record just reset the competitive map — expect sales cycles and pricing across the sector to reference this combined platform's scale from here on.

IRAN

Iran's IRGC Says Three Oil Tankers Ablaze After Hitting Naval Mines in the Strait of Hormuz

Al Jazeera · aljazeera.com ↗

Iran's Revolutionary Guard said three oil tankers caught fire after striking naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, with vessel names, flags and casualties still unconfirmed, as Qatar's prime minister warned the region “cannot withstand another escalation” between the US and Iran. Mined tankers in the world's most important oil chokepoint is a direct hit to shipping and war-risk insurance costs, not background noise — hedging desks and shippers should treat this as the clearest hard-security escalation signal in the region in weeks, regardless of how the surplus narrative is pricing crude.

The Times of Israel · timesofisrael.com ↗

Fed Pricing Swings Toward a December Rate Hike as Traders Await Wednesday's Inflation Data

Fox Business · foxbusiness.com ↗

GAZA CEASEFIRE

Israeli Strikes Kill UNRWA Worker and Two Others in Gaza Despite the Ceasefire

Al Jazeera · aljazeera.com ↗

An Israeli strike near a water well in Jabalia killed UNRWA worker Adel al-Helo and wounded three others, while a separate strike on a vehicle in western Gaza City killed two more, bringing Palestinian deaths since the October 2025 ceasefire to at least 1,679 against five Israeli soldiers. A UN agency worker now among the dead, on top of Kushner's own warning above, confirms the ceasefire is eroding on the ground and not just in coalition politics — raise the odds assigned to a formal ceasefire-collapse scenario accordingly.

UKRAINE

Zelensky Says US Delays on Patriot Transfers Are 'Difficult to Understand' as Russia Fires 165 Missiles in a Week

The Kyiv Independent · kyivindependent.com ↗

Zelensky pressed Washington to approve transfer of NATO-PURL-funded Patriot interceptors that allies have already paid for — Ukraine is averaging just 12 US Patriot interceptors a month in 2026 — after Russia fired roughly 2,700 attack drones, 1,460 glide bombs and 165 missiles in a single week, including a drone strike that killed at least three in Vyshhorod. A US approval bottleneck, not a funding gap, is now the proximate constraint on Ukraine's air defence; any Ukraine-exposure thesis needs to price this hard capacity ceiling rather than the headline peace-talk timeline.

IRAN

US Treasury Escalates Sanctions on Iran, Targeting Its 'Shadow Banking' Networks

Al Jazeera · aljazeera.com ↗

The US Treasury designated more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses and front companies tied to petrochemical exports under “Operation Economic Outcast,” with Secretary Scott Bessent calling the network “the last financial lifeline of a bankrupt regime,” after earlier sanctions pushed Iran's rial to record lows and inflation to roughly 90%. Compliance teams handling any Iranian-linked counterparty or shadow-fleet cargo now face a widened designation net just as Trump reportedly keeps new strikes on the table if talks fail after the new year — treat sanctions exposure here as rising, not static.

Taipei Disputes Xinhua Claim That Trump Told Xi the US 'Does Not Support Taiwan Independence'

Focus Taiwan (CNA) · focustaiwan.tw ↗

Taiwan's foreign ministry said Washington has reaffirmed its Taiwan policy is unchanged, after China's state news agency Xinhua reported that Trump told Xi at their Shenzhen summit the US “does not support Taiwan independence” — a claim the White House has neither confirmed nor denied five days on. An unconfirmed, contested account of what a US president privately told Xi about Taiwan, left unclarified this long, is exactly the kind of ambiguity that moves Taiwan risk premia and allied confidence independent of what was actually said — watch for a US clarification, or the lack of one, as the next signal.

BILL Holdings (SEC filing) · sec.gov ↗

CP24 (The Canadian Press) · cp24.com ↗

ENERGY & OIL

IEA Says the Iran War's Energy-Security Response Could Cut 2035 Oil Demand by Over 4%

Khaleej Times (IEA) · khaleejtimes.com ↗

The IEA's World Energy Outlook says the Iran war has already caused cumulative losses of 2.8 billion barrels of oil and 60 billion cubic metres of gas — the largest-ever disruption to global energy trade — and that an accelerated electrification response could cut 2035 oil demand by 4.4 million barrels a day versus its base case, with chief Fatih Birol saying “the energy world will never be the same.” For oil-major capex planners and long-horizon allocators, this is a structural demand-destruction signal distinct from daily price noise — the war's lasting economic legacy may be an accelerated energy transition, not just a supply shock.

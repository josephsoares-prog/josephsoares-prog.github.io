NVIDIA GOVERNANCE

Bessent Demands Nvidia Look Outside for a Successor, Gives Board 10 Days on Export Compliance

Al Jazeera (Reuters) · aljazeera.com ↗

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Fox News three days after Jensen Huang’s Thursday resignation that Nvidia’s board “need[s] to go outside the company” for a new CEO and demanded answers “in the next 10 days” on whether the company turned a blind eye to illicit chip sales to boost revenue, even as the Financial Times reported Huang’s allies are lobbying Silicon Valley to back a comeback and floated Arm CEO Rene Haas as a rival successor. IRAN-US CONFLICT

Third US Carrier Strike Group Arrives in Middle East as Iran Warns of Pre-Emptive Action

Al Jazeera · aljazeera.com ↗

RUSSIA SANCTIONS

EU Naval Forces Board Russian Shadow-Fleet Tanker in Mediterranean Ahead of New Sanctions Vote

Kyiv Post (TVP World) · kyivpost.com ↗

EU naval forces operating under Operation IRINI boarded the tanker MV Midag in the Mediterranean on Friday under UN maritime law, confirming it was sailing under a false flag as part of Russia’s sanctions-evasion shadow fleet, EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said Sunday, adding “the EU will keep disrupting Russia’s shadow fleet at sea and through diplomatic means.” EU foreign ministers are expected Monday to approve a fresh round of sanctions targeting banks, oil buyers and shadow-fleet enablers. For energy traders and shippers, enforcement has moved from tracking vessels to physically boarding them — raising the compliance cost of touching any cargo with Russian-origin ambiguity.

EUROPE-RUSSIA SECURITY

Estonia Foils GRU-Ordered Sabotage Plot Against Drone Maker Threod Systems

Kyiv Post · kyivpost.com ↗

Estonian security service KAPO and Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said authorities foiled an attempted explosion and arson attack timed for 3:06 a.m. Saturday against domestic drone maker Threod Systems, detaining suspects recruited by Russia’s military intelligence (GRU); Tsahkna said the Kremlin is “increasingly resorting to sabotage” because Ukraine’s resilience “clearly rattled Putin,” and pledged more sanctions and travel restrictions. It is the second such plot against an Estonian defence contractor since an August attack on Milrem Robotics, with the interior minister estimating Russia drives roughly 90% of the country’s internal security threats. For any Western defence-sector supplier, the pattern is now sabotage-as-policy, not an isolated incident.

Kyiv Post · kyivpost.com ↗

Al Jazeera (AP, Reuters) · aljazeera.com ↗

For companies routing trade or energy through contested waters, the 20% left on the table is precisely the part — binding force — that determines whether the code changes behaviour or just paperwork.

April 30, 2026 Politics · Capital Markets · Canada EN · FR
Canada Just Built a Sovereign Wealth Fund Without the Sovereign Wealth
Mark Carney just announced Canada’s first sovereign wealth fund — a $25 billion federal seed inside a $66.9 billion deficit. Norway built theirs from oil surpluses, governed by a fiscal rule no political class can touch. Singapore built theirs from decades of current-account discipline locked in by statute. The Alberta Heritage Fund — the closest Canadian template — was raided into irrelevance the moment a different premier needed cash. The Canada Strong Fund is being announced before the surplus that funds it has been generated and before the legislative anchor that would protect it has been written. That makes it industrial policy in sovereign wealth clothing — a $25B Crown investment vehicle, not a wealth fund. Possibly the right answer for a tariff-pressured economy. Definitely the wrong label. Decision-makers who allocate capital alongside the federal government in the next 90 days should know which one they are actually buying.

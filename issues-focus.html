Issues Focus

Seven tracked segments, each with its own dedicated page: two active wars — Iran and Russia-Ukraine — the interlocking Israel-Lebanon-Palestine-Syria conflict zone, two structural watches, Africa and Europe, and two rising-power watches, Türkiye and China-India-Pakistan. Each brief is read the same way: where things stand, what's actually being decided, who holds the leverage, and what to watch next.

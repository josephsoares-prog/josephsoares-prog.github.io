Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information — and how you stay in control of it.

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 (replaces the version of April 9, 2026)

1. Who we are

This website and the services described on it are operated by IBPROM Corp., a corporation incorporated under the laws of Canada, doing business as Joseph Soares & Co. ("we," "us," "our"). Our head office is at 1 Rideau Street, Suite 700, Ottawa, Ontario K1N 8S7, Canada.

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit josephsoares.com and its related pages (including brief.josephsoares.com), why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose. If anything is unclear, write to us at the address in section 12.

Person responsible for the protection of personal information: Joseph Soares, President, IBPROM Corp. — info@josephsoares.com.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Sign-up forms (for example, the free chapter of Leadership Under Fire, the Corridor Intelligence brief, or an assessment): your first name, email address and, where the form asks for it, your telephone number.
  • Applications and bookings (for example, an application for a conversation, a program or an assessment): your name, contact details, company, role, and the answers you give to the questions on the form.
  • Correspondence: emails, messages and call notes exchanged with us.
  • Payments: if you buy something, the payment itself is handled by our payment processor (section 4). We receive confirmation of the payment, not your full card number.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Analytics: with your consent, Google Analytics 4 records pages viewed, time on page, referral source, approximate location, and device and browser type.
  • Cookies and similar tools: we ask for your consent before setting any cookie that is not strictly necessary. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie banner.
  • Server logs: IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps, kept for security and troubleshooting.
  • Email engagement: whether an email we sent was delivered, opened or clicked, so that we can stop writing to people who have stopped reading.

3. Why we collect it, and on what basis

  • To deliver what you asked for: the chapter, the brief, the assessment result, the reply to your application.
  • To send you the follow-up emails that go with that request, and — only with your consent — occasional notes about the book, Corridor Intelligence and our advisory work. Every email carries an unsubscribe link.
  • Telephone numbers: if you give us your number, we may call or text you about the request you made, or to arrange a conversation you applied for. We do not run automated marketing text campaigns to that number without a separate, explicit consent, and we do not call unannounced for sales purposes.
  • To run, secure and improve the website and our services.
  • To meet legal obligations, including tax and record-keeping.

Where consent is the basis, you can withdraw it at any time (section 6). Withdrawing consent does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

4. Who sees your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

We use a small number of service providers to run the website and send email. Each one processes your information only on our instructions:

  • HighLevel (LeadConnector) — sign-up forms, contact records, and the email and text-message sequences that follow a sign-up. Servers in the United States.
  • Ghost and Mailgun — the Corridor Intelligence brief and its subscriber list. Servers in Canada (Ghost) and the United States (Mailgun).
  • Make and Zapier — routing of form submissions between the systems above and our records. Servers in the European Union and the United States.
  • Google — analytics (with consent), calendar booking, and email hosting. Servers in the United States and elsewhere.
  • Stripe and Wise — payment processing. They hold your payment details under their own privacy policies; we do not.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of the website. Server logs may be processed in the United States.

Because several of these providers are outside Quebec and outside Canada, your information may be stored and processed in the United States and the European Union, where it is subject to the laws of those places. We choose providers that offer contractual privacy and security commitments, and we limit what each one receives to what its job requires.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Subscribers: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your record, after which the address is kept only on a suppression list so that we do not write to you again.
  • Applications, bookings and correspondence: three years after our last exchange, unless an engagement follows, in which case the engagement's own records apply.
  • Client engagement records: seven years, as tax and professional record-keeping require.
  • Analytics: fourteen months in Google Analytics, then aggregated.
  • Server logs: ninety days.

6. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy in a usable format.
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, subject to what the law requires us to keep.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing emails or texts at any time: use the unsubscribe link in any email, reply STOP to any text, or write to us.
  • Object to a use you consider unreasonable, and have a person — not an automated system — review the decision.

Write to info@josephsoares.com. We answer within thirty days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Cookies and analytics: change your choice from the cookie banner at any time, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Our website does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Complaints: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca). If you live in Quebec, you may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca). If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Information travels to us over HTTPS. Access to contact records is limited to Joseph Soares and to the systems named in section 4, each protected by its own credentials. We review access and rotate credentials on a schedule. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever created a real risk of harm to you, we would notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

8. Where we operate, and the laws we follow

  • Canada: the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA); Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), under which every commercial email we send identifies the sender, gives a working unsubscribe, and carries our mailing address; and, for Quebec residents, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector as amended by Law 25.
  • United States: the CAN-SPAM Act for email, the Telephone Consumer Protection Act for calls and texts, and applicable state privacy laws, including California's CCPA/CPRA. We do not sell or "share" personal information in the CCPA sense.
  • European Union and United Kingdom: if you are there, the GDPR and UK GDPR apply to the information we hold about you, and the rights in section 6 are yours in full.

9. Children

Our website and services are for business leaders and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other sites

The website links to LinkedIn, YouTube, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon and other third-party sites. Their privacy practices are their own. This policy covers only information collected through our own properties.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change would materially affect how we use information you have already given us, we tell subscribers by email before it takes effect.

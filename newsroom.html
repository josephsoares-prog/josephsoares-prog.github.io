About Corridor Intelligence

Joseph Soares served in Canada’s Prime Minister’s Office during the 2008 financial crisis and was Chief of Staff in the Senate of Canada during the pandemic. He now runs a management advisory firm that helps businesses and governments work through high-stakes challenges. Corridor Intelligence is his analysis program on power, institutions and decision-making, published in English and French.
